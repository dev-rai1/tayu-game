# TAYU Current Walkthrough and Demo Script v9

Updated: August 1, 2026

This document describes the behavior currently intended for `tayufinance.app`. Use **module** consistently. Do not describe activities as phases or worlds when speaking to students, teachers, or testers.

## Complete route

1. Landing page
2. Log In or Sign Up
3. Pre-game money check-in
4. Grade recommendation or teacher-assigned module path
5. Character creation
6. First-session movement and action practice
7. Module 1: Market & Three Jars
8. Module 2: Lemonade Stand
9. Module 3: Budget Town
10. Module 4: Bank of TAYU
11. Module 5: Money Garden Parts 1 and 2
12. Money Guru finale or an accurate shorter-path certificate

## Account entry

TAYU is free, uses fictional game money, has no in-app purchases, and never asks students to connect a bank account or payment card.

Players enter through **Log In** or **Sign Up**. Guest Mode is no longer a public entry option. Independent testers should choose **Playing on my own**. School or organization users should select the organization option and provide the requested class information.

An account saves learning progress, badges, module checks, settings, and the selected avatar.

## Learning path

Grade level provides a recommendation, not a restriction, for independent learners. Individual users may choose any module. Teacher-controlled classroom assignments and sequencing remain enforceable.

Each module card can show Start, Resume, Done, or Play again. Completed modules can be replayed without erasing badges or prior completion.

## Character creation

Only a valid name is required. Appearance choices are optional. Learners can use the current character, generate a surprise character, shuffle the look, or open more appearance controls.

## Controls and guidance

New players first practice movement and then complete a real interaction. The old instant “Skip to game” control has been removed. An accessibility and experienced-player option appears only after the learner has had time to see the instructions.

Desktop uses WASD and E. Touch devices use the visible MOVE pad and DO button. Store objects and checkout may also be tapped directly.

Only one main instructional surface should be prominent during gameplay. Optional hints begin collapsed and can be hidden, dismissed, or read aloud. The world menu lets a learner restart or leave a confusing module.

## Module 1: Market & Three Jars

Learners allocate money among Spend, Save, and Give. Multiple financially sound allocations are accepted rather than requiring one exact split. Corrective feedback explains the financial goal and one useful next change.

At the market, items display prices and NEED or WANT labels. Learners can tap items and checkout directly. Shopping guidance requires needs before optional wants and rolls unused Spend money into Save.

An optional age-banded bonus-math activity appears after the module without affecting completion or the badge.

## Module 2: Lemonade Stand

Learners choose supplies, hours, recipe, promotion, and price, then observe customers, leftovers, missed demand, costs, tax, and profit.

Weak rounds identify one primary adjustment at a time, such as price, batch size, open hours, recipe quality, or promotion. Guidance does not tell the learner to change several controls simultaneously.

## Module 3: Budget Town

Budget Town is a stationary household decision scene, not a building-to-building walking activity. Learners cover needs, consider a want, divide remaining money among Pocket, Bank, and Money Garden, and test the plan against a surprise expense.

Results remain player-controlled long enough to read and include read-aloud support.

## Module 4: Bank of TAYU

Learners compare account access and growth, distinguish debit from credit, observe borrowing and interest, identify trustworthy debt help, and practice scam safety. A Trust Meter records progress through the lessons.

## Module 5: Money Garden

Money Garden contains ten decisions split into two parts. Part 1 covers investing foundations. Part 2 covers markets, risk, patience, and rebalancing. Learners can save and exit at the intermission and resume at the correct checkpoint.

Prompts ask learners to use evidence rather than revealing an exact company or trade.

## Checks, recaps, and rewards

Each completed module has two short conceptual questions with immediate supportive explanations. The recap uses saved game values. A named collectible is revealed and appears on the Money Guru finale shelf.

## Reading and accessibility

Younger and Older reader settings adjust automatic caption timing. Core instructional cards remain learner-controlled and support Read aloud. A separate Money Word Help guide gives short definitions outside active gameplay.

## Certificates and finale

Shorter teacher-assigned paths receive certificates listing only the modules actually completed. The full five-module journey leads to the Money Guru finale. Certificate display, PDF, and sharing use `tayufinance.app`.

## Required manual validation

Before a public demo, test the complete path on a physical phone and iPad in portrait and landscape. Also test a narrow desktop or compressed meeting window. Confirm that controls do not cover actions, the 3D view never becomes half blank, direct market tapping works, long text scrolls, and saved module and avatar progress returns correctly.
