# TAYU
## Exact Walkthrough & Full Script — Current Build — For Review

This is the complete play-by-play of the current game: every major message box quoted exactly, in the order the player sees it, with a short note (▸) describing what happens at each step. Speaker lines show who is talking. Box IDs let reviewers comment on any exact line. Dynamic values such as `[Name]`, `$[X]`, `[N]%`, and week totals fill in from the player's real choices at runtime.

This reflects the current circular-town journey: **Landing → Login / Guest Mode → Money Check-In → Avatar → Market → Lemonade Stand → Budget Town → Bank → Money Garden → Finale.**

## 2. Module 2 — The Lemonade Stand (Earning)

▸ The child buys supplies from Mr. Bham: Small $4, Medium $6, Large $9, or Mega $12.

▸ Before the first price decision, a two-card help lesson explains the pricing formula. It can be reopened in later weeks.

**L4a (price formula, card 1)** “How do you set your price? Add your costs. Supplies: $6 (cups, lemons, sugar, water, table). Your work: $4 (you can change what your time is worth). Total cost: $6 + $4 = $10.”

▸ The stand panel lets the child set price, hours, and pay. Later weeks unlock quality, a marketing sign, and town news.

▸ At the end of each week, four cards appear in a fixed order.

**L5a (week result — sales)** “Week [N] is done!”

**L5b (sales line)** “Nice work, [Name]! [X] kids bought your lemonade at $[Y] a cup.”

**L5c (quiet-day alternate)** “A quiet day. Nobody bought at $[Y]. Every seller has days like this.”

**L6 (ledger order)** “Revenue → Supplies → Your pay → Profit → Tax (10% of profit) → You keep”

**L7 (goal tracker)** “Goal: $30. You have $[X], $[Y] to go!”

▸ The final card gives one direct, concrete change based on the actual week.

**L8a [Penny — price example]** “Your price is too high this week. Try around $1.05-$1.30 next week.”

**L8b [Penny — supplies example]** “You sold out and 5 kids were turned away. Buy Large supplies next week.”

**L8c [Penny — perfect week]** “PERFECT combination! Supplies match your sales, your price works, your hours pay off. Nothing to fix!”

▸ After the first perfect week, one new feature unlocks per week.

**L9a (quality unlock)** “Something new this week! Upgrade time: EXTRA LEMONY costs more to make, but kids may pay more for it. Or go LESS SUGAR - healthier AND cheaper to make! Your call.”

**L9b (sign unlock)** “Something new this week! A marketing SIGN brings more kids to your stand. Signs cost money - is it worth it? Your call.”

**L9c (news unlock)** “Something new this week! TOWN NEWS is live: each week, something happens in town. Check the news before you decide anything!”

**L10b (swim choice)** “What a swim! But the stand earned nothing today. Everything you choose costs the thing you did not choose.”
