# TAYU Current Validation Checklist

Updated: August 1, 2026

This checklist replaces the obsolete July 30 stacked-PR tracker. The earlier PRs listed as open in that document have already been resolved. Use this file to verify the current build rather than to track old branch order.

## Account and entry

- [ ] Landing page clearly states that TAYU is free, uses fictional money, has no in-app purchases, and requires no bank or card connection.
- [ ] Log In, Sign Up, and Forgot Password are the only public account-entry options.
- [ ] Independent testers can identify **Playing on my own** without verbal help.
- [ ] School and organization users can enter the correct class information.
- [ ] New non-teacher accounts land on the TAYU home page.

## Character creation

- [ ] Quick-start actions remain above optional customization on phone and tablet widths.
- [ ] Only the player name is required.
- [ ] Use This Character, Surprise Me & Enter, and Shuffle the Look all work.
- [ ] Saved avatars render correctly after returning to the game.
- [ ] Earned module rewards have a visible avatar payoff once the reward PR is merged.

## Navigation and overlays

- [ ] First-session movement and action practice appears once.
- [ ] The old immediate “Skip to game” control is absent.
- [ ] The delayed accessibility/experienced-player option appears after the instruction has been visible.
- [ ] MOVE and DO controls do not block cards, menus, or buttons.
- [ ] Only one prominent instructional or hint surface appears at a time.
- [ ] Optional hints can be hidden, dismissed, and read aloud.
- [ ] Players can restart or leave a module through the world menu.
- [ ] Off-screen objective guidance points toward the correct destination.

## Responsive 3D world

- [ ] The player cannot leave the circular playable island.
- [ ] Turning or walking toward the map edge never produces a half-blank or split-looking world.
- [ ] The canvas fills the viewport after phone browser-chrome changes, orientation changes, split-screen resizing, tab restoration, and ordinary resize.
- [ ] All five modules and the finale remain reachable.

## Module 1: Market & Three Jars

- [ ] Jar feedback is large enough to notice and explains one useful next action.
- [ ] Multiple financially sound allocations are accepted.
- [ ] The ghost split is presented as an example rather than the only correct answer.
- [ ] Market objects show price and NEED or WANT labels.
- [ ] Items and checkout work through direct tap/click as well as E or DO.
- [ ] Shopping before Mr. Bram and selecting an existing basket item produce clear feedback.
- [ ] Optional age-banded bonus math matches the saved grade band.

## Module 2: Lemonade Stand

- [ ] Supplies, hours, recipe, promotion, price, customers, leftovers, missed demand, costs, tax, and profit remain connected.
- [ ] A weak round identifies only one primary lever to change first.
- [ ] Price, shortage, leftovers, hours, recipe, and promotion corrections are understandable.
- [ ] Retry guidance does not reveal a complete winning plan.

## Module 3: Budget Town

- [ ] Budget Town remains a stationary household scene.
- [ ] Needs and optional wants are clear.
- [ ] Pocket, Bank, and Money Garden tradeoffs are understandable.
- [ ] Surprise-expense results remain visible until the learner continues.
- [ ] Read aloud works on the main takeaway.

## Module 4: Bank of TAYU

- [ ] Checking, savings, CD, debit, credit, interest, debt help, and scam safety remain intact.
- [ ] Trust progress is visible.
- [ ] Scam guidance encourages pausing and asking a trusted adult.

## Module 5: Money Garden

- [ ] Ten decisions remain split into two five-decision parts.
- [ ] Save and exit resumes at the correct Part 2 checkpoint.
- [ ] Evidence prompts do not reveal an exact company or trade.
- [ ] The generic coach does not overlap the specialized investing guide.

## Completion, replay, and rewards

- [ ] Every module routes through exactly two conceptual questions.
- [ ] Immediate feedback is supportive and explanatory.
- [ ] Personalized recaps use actual saved values.
- [ ] Completed modules can be replayed without erasing completion.
- [ ] Shorter paths receive accurate certificates.
- [ ] Full-path completion reaches the Money Guru finale.

## Reading and accessibility

- [ ] Younger and Older reader settings persist.
- [ ] Automatic captions scale with word count.
- [ ] Core lesson cards remain learner-controlled.
- [ ] Money Word Help is available outside active gameplay.
- [ ] Keyboard focus, live-region announcements, scrolling, and text wrapping remain usable.

## Required human validation

- [ ] Complete Module 1 on a physical iPad in portrait and landscape.
- [ ] Complete the full game on a physical phone.
- [ ] Test a narrow or compressed meeting window.
- [ ] Retest with at least one original student tester.
- [ ] Run separate elementary, middle-school, and high-school observations.
- [ ] Review analytics after real sessions for retries, time, drop-off, and unclear steps.

A software change is not considered fully validated until the relevant physical-device and student-observation checks are complete.
