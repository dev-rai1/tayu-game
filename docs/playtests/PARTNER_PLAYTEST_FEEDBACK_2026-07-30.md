# Partner Playtest Feedback - July 30, 2026

## Feedback received

> I spent about 3-5 minutes on every module, and closer to 8-9 on the investing one. The content in the modules was great, but there were some bugs. For instance, in the budget module, some chats were automatically skipped, and in investing, the hints would get covered by the week's objective. Overall, it's a great game that might just need a few UI improvements. We think it could be a good fit for us to include in our presentations as a suggestion for students to engage outside of class, where we could give a short description of the game and why people should play it.

## What this confirms

- Typical observed time was approximately **3-5 minutes per module**.
- The Investing / Money Garden module took approximately **8-9 minutes**, which is consistent with its two-part, ten-decision structure.
- The reviewer considered the educational content strong.
- The game is being considered as an **outside-of-class student engagement recommendation** in presentations rather than as a required in-class activity.

## Bugs identified and response

### Budget Town outcome chats advanced too quickly

Some instructional outcome text was presented as short-lived toast content. The next decision card could appear while that text was still active, causing it to feel automatically skipped.

**Response:** the six core Budget Town takeaways are now captured in a player-controlled result dialog with **Read aloud** and **Continue**. Animation-only messages remain brief so the module does not become unnecessarily slow.

### Investing hint was covered by the weekly objective

The persistent mission chip and the pinned Investing lesson hint could compete for the same upper-screen space.

**Response:** the mission chip now hides only during the Investing `adjust` and initial `slider` phases, when the pinned lesson hint is the more important instruction. It returns in other Investing phases and modules.

## Presentation positioning

Suggested short description:

**TAYU is a free, game-based financial literacy experience where students practice budgeting, saving, earning, banking, and investing through choices and visible consequences. Most modules take only a few minutes, and students can continue or replay them outside of class.**

Suggested reason to play:

**Students do not just read definitions - they make financial decisions, see what changes, receive supportive feedback, and can revisit modules at their own pace.**
